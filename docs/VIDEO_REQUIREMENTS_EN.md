# Winter 2026 challenge video: official requirements vs recommended production

Official Winter Stage 1: **NO mandatory video**. Required are flash/SSH evidence (screenshot/composite), sensor evidence (photo/screenshot/short clip), AI evidence (screenshot on RDK X5), personal public GitHub README, personal Discord Stage 1 permalink, and one showcase PR under `projects/`.

An optional **90–150-second** Stage 1 video could show: (1) 0–12s actual board/camera, (2) 12–35s flash+SSH, (3) 35–55s USB/V4L2 capture, (4) 55–95s live YOLO11m on RDK X5, (5) 95–130s repository and evidence links. Suggested duration, NOT an official limit.

Official Winter Stage 3: **MANDATORY public demo video**, **3–7 minutes**, **1080p preferred**, must include hardware, visible output, and **at least 30 uninterrupted seconds of on-device AI operation**. A stock Stage 1 YOLO demo is not enough to satisfy Stage 3's integrated project + BPU proof + benchmark requirements.

Stage 1 rules: https://github.com/D-Robotics/Robotics-Dream-Keeper-Challenge-Winter/blob/main/stages/stage1-ignite.md

Stage 3 rules: https://github.com/D-Robotics/Robotics-Dream-Keeper-Challenge-Winter/blob/main/stages/stage3-launch.md

Preserve genuine raw evidence. Do not publish passwords, private network details, device serial numbers, or keys. Do not fake hardware demonstrations.
